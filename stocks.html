How to read this page

Every symbol gets the same treatment: up to ten years of daily closes go into Google TimesFM 2.5 (200M parameters, zero shot), which emits a 5 session forecast path. “Next %” is the forecast for the next trading session versus the latest close; “5 session %” is the horizon end forecast. Direction hit is the model's one session ahead direction accuracy over the last 10 sessions for that symbol, and RMSE % is its typical size of error relative to price; smaller is better. The small line charts show the last 60 sessions of closes. Every label on this page that might be unfamiliar links to the glossary.

These are offline artifacts, refreshed by the documented procedure in the README; the 800 MB model checkpoint cannot run inside the serverless runtime. Each row carries its own as of date, and the age of the artifact is shown in the header. Forecasts are model outputs, not advice. Zero shot models do not know about earnings dates, guidance or news.
