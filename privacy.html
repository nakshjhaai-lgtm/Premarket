Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

The short version

No accounts. No cookies. No analytics. No advertising. No third party trackers. The only thing stored in your browser is one localStorage key remembering your light/dark theme choice.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The site is static pages plus serverless functions that fetch public market data. We do not ask for your name, email, or any identifier, and we do not fingerprint your device.

Server logs

The hosting platform (Netlify) keeps standard request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention, as described in Netlify's own privacy policy. We do not access, analyze, or sell these logs.

Local storage

  • pm-theme: your theme preference (“light” or “dark”). Stored in your browser only; never transmitted to us.

Clearing your browser storage removes it. No other client side storage is used.

Third party data providers

Market data is fetched server side by our cloud functions from CNBC, Yahoo Finance, NSE India and TradingView. Your browser never contacts these providers through this site, and they do not receive your IP from us. Displayed data remains the property of its providers and is shown for research purposes only.

Children

The site is a financial research tool, not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes materially (for example, if analytics were ever added; they won't be without a policy update first), this page and its “last updated” date will change before the feature ships.
