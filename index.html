What this is

A decision framework, not a vote counter. At 09:00 IST every trading day a cloud function builds the opening bias from GIFT Nifty's implied gap, checks it against global cues (US close, live US futures, Asia) and the NSE pre open session, gauges risk from India VIX against its own 1 year range, and runs a 3 layer LSTM trained on 18 years of NIFTY history blended with a zero shot Google TimesFM forecast. At 09:20 IST a confirmation pass records what price action actually did. No accounts, no tracking, no upsell. Sources are listed on every row. Each source has automatic fallbacks, and their state is shown above. When everything fails you see the word unavailable, never a guess. Every term links to the glossary, and the whole site has a simple explanation page.
