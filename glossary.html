How this list is kept

Every term that appears anywhere on this site is meant to have an entry here. Terms link to each other where one helps explain another, and the dashboard, the stocks page and the model page link their jargon straight to the matching entry. If you ever meet a term on this site that is neither defined on the spot nor present here, that is a bug and it gets fixed. The sibling page, ELI5, explains the whole site in the simplest words first, and this glossary holds the precise definitions.

Definitions describe how each term is used on this site specifically. For general encyclopedic meanings, treat a search engine or a dictionary as the next stop. Nothing in this glossary is investment advice.
